## SWAG Is A Love Letter In Song

The most obvious thing one gets from this album on the first listen is that it is about JB's wife, or at least the relationship they have.  

I'd say the most impressive thing about this LP is how JB manages to use stripped-down production, soft vocals, and deep-cutting lyrics to communicate softness throughout the album.  

The album starts with [ALL I CAN TAKE](https://open.spotify.com/track/72PVbRKkUimweUPV0nKAPs?si=00103a1913144a23), which talks about letting loose and taking a chance on being with someone despite being flawed, an optimistic way to a start of something special.  

The lyrics below give that idea:  

<LyricsTile href='https://genius.com/Justin-bieber-all-i-can-take-lyrics'>
    There's things that I can't change, Lord knows I've tried, mm
    Ooh, baby, we can leave it all behind, uh
</LyricsTile>

In these few lines, JB is able to show he has his flaw but he is willing to leave it all behind for this relationship. In my eyes (and ears), this is a good way to start such an album.

## The Good, GO BABY like Songs

Throughout the album, there are songs like [DAISIES](https://open.spotify.com/track/5BZsQlgw21vDOAjoqkNgKb?si=7389400068af4a23), [YUKON](https://open.spotify.com/track/29iva9idM6rFCPUlu7Rhxl?si=7a17538f74d44d43), and [GO BABY](https://open.spotify.com/track/01p5urrGw5fuFCcfT7PBgc?si=fbf3f59ff2b54c6f) that serve as a celebration of the love JB shares with his wife.  

I really love how **DAISIES** is the second song in the LP and you hear lyrics like `"Counting days 'till I can see you again"`, showing JB's excitement of being in this relationship.
This really captures the early days of a relationship, what some would refer to a honeymoon phase, just loving every moment of being with someone.  

**YUKON** was the one song that caught my attention at first from these lovey-dovey songs, mainly because of the autotuned JB vocals, 2Chainz, and the acoustic bass guitar, MY GOD!! WHAAAAT!!  

## Intimacy Is The Songs

I think two songs capture the intimacy JB was going for, [THINGS YOU DO](https://open.spotify.com/track/1j8TmNa6vaFAEAws43kP0Q?si=81f9aefd67a74061) and [DEVOTION](https://open.spotify.com/track/4uyBm26e7ElRigCH6vRbpc?si=7567b2119d524e20). In both songs, we get to have JB's vocal over simple acoustic instruments. 

I mean, just listen to **DEVOTION** when it starts, those hi-hats and cymbals, OMG, the drums in this song are just incredible:

    embedUrl='https://www.youtube.com/embed/SpbDwr7HZdw?si=v7-JIDVSLSs8PqEB'

It almost feels like you are in the room that the song is recorded in. You hear background noises and mic being adjusted, the choir in the background at the end of DEVOTION — what a beautiful song.  

In **THINGS YOU DO** JB sings these lines:  

<LyricsTile href='https://genius.com/Justin-bieber-things-you-do-lyrics'>
    Let's go for a drive, I know that you like it, let's do it again
    So do it again like you did when we were together (When we were together)
    Please just touch my back, oh, like you love to do, oh
    The little things you do, oh, that drive, drivin' me crazy, oh, yeah

Reminding us that the smallest things like `"Please just touch my back"` are enough to make one feel so in love. The bass guitar softly echoing in the background also just touches your soul.   

One other thing JB and his production team did really well is being able to make you hear the acoustic nature of it all: the guitar strings slowly fading, and you hear this sudden stop, like someone put their hand on the strings to stop the strings' vibrations, thus giving JB's voice more room to shine — what a wow!!

## Love Is Hard But We Can Choose Softness 

I think one thing that puts a cherry on top in this LP is that there are songs that talk about the hardship of a relationship.  

How Justin Bieber does it is with grace, knowing disagreements are inevitable, but we can work together in this.  

The songs that capture this for me are [FIRST PLACE](https://open.spotify.com/track/4ZZJ7S4Z1pEIvTUgN1oRzS?si=68c0c270fe544929) and [WALKING AWAY](https://open.spotify.com/track/0LutvoC2HqCfnaRSjrhtzo?si=1dae80be17374d50). A lyric so profound in WALKING AWAY for me is:

<LyricsTile href='https://genius.com/Justin-bieber-walking-away-lyrics'>
    Change takes time, girl
    Let's see what we can do, uh (We can do, we can do, we can do, we can do)
    'Cause even when they leave
    I keep on dreamin' of the best parts of you, oh
</LyricsTile>

This is the grace I was talking about, choosing to see the good in your partner even in moments where that goodness is not shown.
Dreaming about the parts even when they leave, that is actively loving someone. It's not comfortable or easy, but you choose it regardless.  

Songs like this make me believe in LOVE, not walking away, staying for the sake of your partner, in the songs, JB adds `"You are my diamond, gave you a ring, told you I'd change"`.

This man reassures his wife that the diamond on the ring is not the real precious thing in this relationship, but YOU ARE! MY GOOD LORD WOW!!

I honestly wish everyone to experience such love. The love that doesn't give up on you, EVER!

## The End

Reviewing the entire LP would be hard over one blog post, but I wish more people gave this LP the love it deserves.  

It's honestly one of my favorite albums of all time. It really does have some soul in it, a lot of it too.
